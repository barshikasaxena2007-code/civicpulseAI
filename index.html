⚖️ Transparent Priority Rules

CivicPulse AI does not assign priorities arbitrarily. Our rule-based priority engine evaluates each request against three objective factors:

  • 1
    Urgency/Severity: Critical threats to health and life receive top initial weighting.
  • 2
    People Affected: Issues affecting 200+ citizens receive greater priority than single-household concerns.
  • 3
    Category Impact: Drinking water and public safety take precedence over cosmetic maintenance.
Note: Every submitted request immediately calculates its priority score and appears on the government dashboard.
